
## 1. What the product is

AncientGroveOS is the operating record for an olive estate. By the end of all phases it answers, at any moment:

- What is each tree, where is it, how is it doing?
- What was done in the grove this week, this month, this year — and by whom? *(Phase 1+)*
- What pests, diseases, or stress signs are present, and where? *(Phase 1+)*
- What did each tree yield, and what was the oil quality of the batch it ended up in? *(Phase 2+)*
- Given any bottle, which trees produced its oil? And given any tree, which bottles carry its oil? *(Phase 3+)*
- What inputs (water, fertilizer, sprays) were applied, when, where, in what dose? *(Phase 1+)*
- Are inherited heritage practices (Ba'al, Souri, late harvest, press-within-hours) holding up under current conditions? *(growing answer, all phases)*

**Phase 0 answers:** What is each tree, where is it, what does it look like, what variety, what age — and what's the baseline state of the estate as a record?

---

## 2. Why this exists

Existing orchard apps (Croptracker, Farmbrite, Leaftide) are good at compliance, spray records, and yield reports, but they treat trees as a unit of accounting. Heritage olive groves have **named, photographed, individual trees** that live for centuries and carry agronomic and cultural identity. AncientGroveOS keeps tree identity at the centre of the record.

A second gap, which later phases fill: most tools handle harvest as a single number. An olive estate generating premium oil needs to trace **box → harvest event → tree → batch → press → oil → bottle**. Without that chain, neither growers nor customers can connect a bottle back to the trees that filled it.

A third gap: heritage practices are usually invisible to software. Here they are first-class — measurable, reviewable, and adapted over time.

## 3. Goals

1. **Single record per tree.** Identity, location, geometry, photos, observations, treatments, harvest history, evidence links.
2. **Single record per grove.** Boundary, agronomic profile, year-over-year status.
3. **Operational closeness.** Daily and weekly views the Manager actually opens; *(Phase 1)* field forms an Operations Manager can complete in under a minute per tree.
4. **Full traceability from tree to bottle.** *(Phase 2–3)* Every bottle can be traced back to the trees that filled it; every tree can list the bottles it contributed to.
5. **Honest evidence layering.** Satellite signals are signals, lab results are batch-level unless tree-sampled, sensor data is contextual until interpreted. The UI never collapses these distinctions.
6. **Sensor-ready model.** *(Phase 4)* Soil moisture, weather, dendrometer, and trap-counter data attach cleanly when the hardware arrives.
7. **Heritage practices as living rules.** Inherited practices have data, status, and adaptation pressure visible in the system.
8. **Role-appropriate autonomy.** The Operations Manager *(Phase 1)* runs the grove year-round without daily task assignments; the Manager reviews and intervenes only on strategic calls.

**Goal for Phase 0 specifically:** a clean, complete, browsable initial database — every tree on the estate has a record with location, variety, photos, and basic identity, queryable on a map and in lists.

### 4.1 Operations Manager — autonomy *(Phase 1)*

The Operations Manager is **not a worker waiting for assignments**. They know the year's rhythm, decide their own daily plan, and execute. The Manager does not assign tasks day-to-day; they review what happened and can flag specific trees for attention.

In the Phase 1 build:
- The Operations App opens on a self-managed plan, not an assigned task list.
- The Manager can flag items inline ("satellite shows low vigor on T-0142, please inspect"); flags are surfaced alongside the OM's own plan, not in place of it.
- Activity, scouting, and treatment records are the OM's primary outputs.

### 4.2 Permissions

| Role | Read | Write |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manager | All | All |
| Operations Manager *(Phase 1)* | All operational records; trees and groves; activity history | Field visits, scouting, pest/disease finds, trap counts, treatments, pruning and activity records, phenology events, photos, new-tree records, damage reports |
| Harvest Worker *(Phase 2)* | Today's harvest assignments and the trees on them | Harvest events and boxes for assigned trees |

Manager-only writes: lab result entry, oil batch creation, bottling runs, heritage rule status changes, tree status moves to `removed`.

All destructive edits create an audit event.

## 6. Modules

The trackers in §5 are populated through these modules. The phase tag indicates when each module first ships in the product.

### 6.1 Grove Registry *(Phase 0)*
Boundary geometry, area, slope, orientation, terraces, soil profile, planting layout, heritage notes (cultivar, family history, terrace style, age estimate). Aggregate counts and status surfaces.

### 6.2 Tree Registry *(Phase 0)*
Searchable index of every tree. Tree detail page is the heart of the product. In Phase 0 the page shows:

- Map with tree highlighted in grove context.
- Photo timeline (geotagged, dated; swipeable).
- Identity (code, variety, age estimate, ancient status, planting date if known).
- Crown polygon overlay + crown area.
- Latest satellite-derived metrics (NDVI etc., per imagery date).
- Verification status.
- Notes and tags.
- Audit trail.

Sections that fill in later phases: geometry & growth measurements (Phase 1), field visits (Phase 1), pest finds & treatments (Phase 1), harvest history (Phase 2), bottles produced (Phase 3), sensor readings (Phase 4).

**Tree types:** olive, fig, carob, other, unknown. Fig and carob coexist with olives because of trap-tree and soil-partner heritage rules.

**Field identification:** GPS proximity match (default radius 5 m) + manual confirmation against the latest photo. Optional `field_tag` for QR or painted code. Detailed in [VISUAL_TREE_INTELLIGENCE.md](VISUAL_TREE_INTELLIGENCE.md).

### 6.3 Photo Library *(Phase 0)*
Photos are first-class. Every photo is associated with:

- A tree (or grove, or zone).
- A timestamp.
- GPS at time of capture (when available).
- A purpose tag (`general`, `growth`; later `pre_harvest`, `box`, `pest`, `disease`, `damage`, `pruning_before`, `pruning_after`, `bottling`).
- An optional linked entity.

**Phase 0 capture paths:**
- **Bulk import** — drop a folder of photos into the Manager app; VTI matches each to a tree by EXIF GPS + visual similarity. Manager confirms low-confidence matches.
- **Per-tree upload** — open a tree, add photos directly.

**Browse modes:**
- Per-tree timeline (default tree page).
- Latest photos across the estate (Manager Today view).
- Filter by purpose tag.

**On capture/import:** EXIF GPS and timestamp extracted, original retained, 512px thumbnail generated.

### 6.4 Phenology & Calendar *(Phase 1, seeded earlier)*
A grove-year calendar showing the BBCH stages and the events that bound them. In Phase 0, the calendar exists as a structure with no events logged; the Manager can log historical first-rain or harvest dates from memory if useful. From Phase 1 onwards, the OM logs events in real time.

### 6.5 Field Activity Log *(Phase 1)*
What was done, where, by whom, when. Authored by the Operations Manager. Activity types include pruning (light / structural / heavy), mowing / understory management, terrace repair, stone work / drainage, cover crop seeding, tree planting / replanting, tree removal, inspection / scouting, cleaning / clearing, damage assessment, other.

### 6.6 Pest & Disease Scouting *(Phase 1)*
A targeted form for the olive pest and disease set:

| Pest / disease | Scouting cue | Treatment relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Olive fruit fly (*Bactrocera oleae*) | Trap counts; sting marks on fruit | Threshold-based treatment, harvest timing |
| Peacock spot (*Spilocaea oleagina*) | Dark circular spots on leaves; defoliation | Copper sprays, prune for airflow |
| Verticillium wilt | Wilting, yellowing, vascular browning | Soil management, tolerant cultivars |
| Olive moth (*Prays oleae*) | 3 generations: leaf, flower, fruit | Pheromone monitoring, BT treatments |
| Olive knot (*Pseudomonas savastanoi*) | Galls on twigs after wounds | Prune in dry weather, disinfect tools |
| Anthracnose (*Colletotrichum*) | Sunken spots on ripening fruit | Pre-harvest copper, harvest timing |
| Black scale | Sticky honeydew, sooty mould | Biological control |
| Olive psyllid | White waxy secretions | Monitor; rarely needs treatment |
| Other / unknown | Photo + notes | Manager review |

Scouting record fields and the trap module shape are described in detail when Phase 1 begins.

### 6.7 Spray & Fertilizer Records *(Phase 1)*
Every chemical, fertilizer, or biological input applied. Compliance-ready format:

- Product (registered name, active ingredient).
- Rate (per ha or per tree).
- Area / trees treated.
- Method (foliar, soil, drip, dust).
- Date, applicator, weather conditions.
- Withholding interval (days to harvest).
- Notes.
- Photos (optional).

**No approval workflow.** The Operations Manager records treatments as they apply them; the Manager reviews them in the Today view. (An approval gate was considered and explicitly dropped to keep OM autonomous.)

A pre-harvest withholding-period checker warns if any treated grove or tree falls inside its withholding window when a harvest is planned.

### 6.8 Water & Climate *(Phase 1)*
Daily rainfall log (manual until weather station arrives). Annual cumulative, compared to long-term average. Irrigation events for any non-Ba'al grove. First-rain marker. Heat events flagged.

### 6.9 Harvest *(Phase 2 — operated through the Harvest App, §10.3)*
Tree-level harvest workflow:

1. Harvest worker selects tree (GPS-assisted), confirms identity.
2. Captures pre-harvest tree photo.
3. Records workers involved.
4. Adds boxes one by one, each with photo and estimated/measured weight.
5. Optionally samples 100 olives for **Jaén maturity index**.
6. Completes tree harvest. Boxes are added to a batch by the Manager.

### 6.10 Batch, Pressing & Oil *(Phase 2)*
**Batch** groups boxes for the mill. **Pressing run** records mill, times, input weight, output litres, oil yield %, and the **pressing delay** (the operational measure for the Press-Within-Hours rule). **Oil batch** records volume, storage container, and tracks `volume_remaining_litres` for partial bottling.

### 6.11 Lab Results *(Phase 2)*
Default attribution: oil-batch level. Tree-level only when tree-specific sampling occurred — the UI always shows the level. Captured fields: free acidity, peroxide value, K232, K270, ΔK, total polyphenols, oleocanthal, oleacein, fatty acid composition, lab name, sample date, report PDF.

Derived flags: extra virgin (`acidity ≤ 0.8%`), health-claim eligible polyphenols (`≥ 250 mg/kg`).

### 6.12 Bottling & Bottle→Tree Traceability *(Phase 3)*
Bottling runs draw oil from one or more oil batches into bottles of a chosen format with a lot code. The system materializes per-tree contribution shares so any lot code resolves to its contributing trees, and any tree resolves to the bottling runs that include its oil. Per-bottle codes (QR for premium / single-tree releases) are Phase 4. Detailed schema in §8.

### 6.13 Heritage Practices *(seeded Phase 0; evidence accumulates Phase 1+)*
The 9 named rules (First Rain · Ba'al Rainfed · Fig Trap Trees · Carob Soil Partners · Wild Understory · Sanasil Stone Terraces · Late Harvest · Souri Single Cultivar · Press Within Hours) are stored as structured rules with: traditional rule · rationale · scientific hypothesis · required data · climate risk · evidence status · linked records.

In Phase 0 the rules are seeded with their starting status. Evidence linking begins in Phase 1.

### 6.14 AI Grove Assistant *(optional, can ship anytime; not in Phase 0)*
Grounded retrieval over the database. Cites every record. Useful once data accumulates; in Phase 0 the database is too thin for the Assistant to be more useful than direct browsing.

### 6.15 Reports
Reports the Manager actually wants:

- **Registry health report** *(Phase 0)*: how many groves, how many trees, % field-verified, % with at least one photo, by-grove breakdown — answers "is the database actually built?"
- **Tree report** *(Phase 0)*: identity, photo strip, crown geometry, satellite metrics, notes. Phase 1+ adds visit and treatment timeline; Phase 2+ adds harvest history; Phase 3+ adds bottles produced.
- **Year report** *(Phase 1+)*: rainfall vs avg, phenology shifts, pest pressure peaks, treatments, pruning, harvest, oil quality, bottling, heritage rule evidence delta.
- **Harvest report** *(Phase 2+)*: total kg, kg per grove, kg per tree, maturity, pressing delays, oil yield, lab summary.
- **Lot trace report** *(Phase 3+)*: full origin chain from tree photos through to lab numbers for any bottling lot code.
- **Compliance export** *(Phase 1+)*: spray & fertilizer log filtered to a date range and grove (CSV/PDF).

## 7. Manager dashboard (desktop)

### 7.1 Phase 0 Today view — registry building
This is what the Manager opens during the registry-build period.

- **Registry progress:** total groves, total trees, % field-verified, % with at least one photo, recently added trees.
- **Latest photos** added to the library (thumbnail strip).
- **Pending VTI confirmations** (low-confidence photo-to-tree matches awaiting Manager decision).
- **Pending imports** (uploads in progress — bulk tree CSVs, satellite GeoJSONs, photo folders).
- **Open questions on the data:** trees missing variety, age, or photo; trees flagged `disputed` during verification.

### 7.2 Today view as it grows *(Phase 1+)*
From Phase 1 onwards, the Today view also shows OM activity since last login, items needing decisions, today's harvest *(Phase 2)*, trap counts overdue, phenology nudges, withholding watch, overdue items.

### 7.3 Year view *(Phase 1+)*
Grove health distribution by month, rainfall vs long-term average, phenology multi-year overlay, pest pressure curves, field visit coverage, heritage rule evidence accumulation, harvest/oil/bottling summary.

### 7.4 Harvest view *(Phase 2, in season only)*
Live harvest progress, tree harvest map, active events, today's boxes, batches, pressing schedule, maturity sampling.

**Phase 0 active set:** `groves`, `trees`, `imagery_acquisitions`, `tree_satellite_observations`, `media`, `heritage_rules`, `users`, `audit_events`. Everything else is created (schema in place) but not written to until its phase begins.

**Key relationships** (full):
- `grove 1—* tree`
- `tree 1—* tree_geometry_record`, `1—* tree_satellite_observation`, `1—* harvest_event`, `1—* media`
- `harvest_event 1—* harvest_box`
- `harvest_batch 1—* harvest_box`
- `harvest_batch 1—1 pressing_run`
- `pressing_run 1—* oil_batch`
- `oil_batch 1—* lab_result` (and rare `tree 1—* lab_result` only when tree-sampled)
- `oil_batch *—* bottling_run` via `bottling_run_oil_sources`
- `bottling_run *—* tree` via `bottle_origins` materialized view
- `heritage_rule *—* {records}` via `rule_evidence`

## 12. Workflows

A short orientation. The detailed workflow inventory and current-focus framing live in [WORKFLOWS.md](WORKFLOWS.md).

## 13. Phase 0 scope (current build)

### 13.1 In Phase 0

**Frontend:** Manager Command Center (web only).

**Functional scope:**
- Grove registry: create/edit groves with boundary polygon, area, slope, orientation, soil profile, heritage notes.
- Tree registry: create/edit trees with code, GPS, variety, age estimate, ancient flag, planting date, notes, verification status.
- Tree map: all groves and trees plotted; crown polygons rendered from satellite-derived data.
- Photo library: per-tree timeline; bulk-upload from folder of photos; VTI matches photos to trees by EXIF GPS + visual similarity; Manager confirms/corrects low-confidence matches.
- Satellite observation import: CSV/GeoJSON of per-tree NDVI / crown / health values.
- Bulk tree import: CSV/GeoJSON.
- Heritage rules seed: 9 named rules with status `hypothesis` or `monitoring` as appropriate; basic CRUD.
- Registry health report: completeness checks across the database.
- Tree report (Phase 0 fields).
- Audit events on all writes.
- Auth: Manager login (Supabase Auth, email + password).
- English UI.

### 13.2 Not in Phase 0
- Operations App (Phase 1).
- Harvest App (Phase 2).
- Bottling module (Phase 3).
- Treatment approval flow — *removed entirely*; treatments will be recorded by the OM in Phase 1 with no approval gate.
- Field visits, scouting, trap counts, treatments, activity logs (Phase 1).
- Phenology calendar (Phase 1).
- Harvest, batches, pressing runs, oil batches, lab results (Phase 2).
- IoT sensor integration (Phase 4).
- AI photo triage (Phase 4).
- AI Grove Assistant (any phase, optional).
- Push notifications, SMS.
- Multi-estate / multi-tenant.
- Localization beyond English.

### 13.3 Phase 0 acceptance

The Phase 0 build is complete when the Manager can:

1. **Define the estate.** Create at least one grove with a boundary drawn on a map; add basic metadata.
2. **Populate the tree registry.** Add trees one-by-one, *and* via CSV/GeoJSON bulk import, *and* by uploading a folder of geotagged photos that the system maps to the right trees (creating new trees when GPS doesn't match any existing tree, with manual confirmation).
3. **See the estate on a map.** Open the Grove Map, see all groves and trees plotted, click any tree to open its detail page.
4. **Browse a tree's history.** Open a tree detail page; see its photo timeline, identity fields, crown polygon, satellite metrics, notes, and audit trail.
5. **Mark verification.** Walk through trees on the map, mark each as `field-verified` once confirmed (Manager does this from the desktop based on field walkthrough notes — no Operations App needed yet).
6. **Seed heritage rules.** All 9 named rules exist with their starting metadata; can be edited.
7. **Run the registry health report.** See completeness percentages and a list of trees missing key fields.

## 14. Roadmap

**Phase 1 — Field operations** *(after Phase 0 stabilizes)*
- Operations App PWA for the Operations Manager.
- Field visits, scouting, trap counts, treatments, activity logs.
- Phenology calendar with multi-year overlay (overlay starts useful at Phase 2+).
- Manager flag mechanism.
- Today view extended with OM activity, pending decisions, withholding watch.

- Harvest App PWA for harvest workers and the Operations Manager.
- Harvest events, boxes, batches, pressing runs, oil batches, lab results.
- Maturity sampling, pre-harvest photo archive.
- Harvest view in Manager dashboard.
- Year report, harvest report.

**Phase 3 — Bottling & traceability**
- Bottling runs with multi-source oil composition.
- `bottle_origins` materialization.
- Lot trace report (printable).

**Phase 4 — Sensors & AI**
- IoT integration (weather, soil moisture, trap counter, dendrometer, tree camera).
- AI photo triage (pest/disease/maturity).
- Per-bottle QR codes for premium / single-tree releases.

**Phase 5 — Research & multi-estate**
- Public lot trace at `/trace/<lot_code>`.
- Multi-year reports, exportable datasets.
- Multi-estate support.
